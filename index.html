Planning Inside the Twin

TwinTAMP: Real2Sim2Real task and motion planning for deformable and rigid objects

Austine Roy Oloo

Overview

TwinTAMP builds a physics twin of a tabletop scene from a single RGB-D capture, with reconstructed rigid objects and calibrated cloth and rope models. It then plans a whole packing task inside that twin, choosing folds, coils and rigid placements in one search and evaluating each deformable effect with a physics rollout. The plan runs on a real UR5e, and a second capture checks whether the twin predicted the real outcome.
